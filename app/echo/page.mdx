# Echo — AI Communication Copilot

<div className="text-zinc-400 text-sm mb-8 font-serif">
  Persona Case Study • Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) • Local LLMs (Ollama) & Privacy-First Vector Memory
</div>

<div className="space-y-8 text-[15.5px] text-zinc-300 leading-relaxed font-serif">

<strong className="text-zinc-100 font-medium">Echo</strong> is an intelligent browser extension (an AI communication copilot) that integrates directly into your web messaging apps. It learns your personal writing style (favorite greetings, sentence length, vocabulary, and tone habits) and suggests instant, authentic replies right above your chat box — eliminating the stiff, robotic tone typical of standard AI chatbots.

<h2 className="font-cormorant text-2xl text-zinc-100 font-semibold pt-4 mb-2">1. The Problem: Why Standard AI Fails in Messaging</h2>

Most people communicating across personal, professional, and team channels face four critical frictions:

<ol className="space-y-3 text-zinc-300 pl-5 list-decimal">
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">The "Robotic AI" Syndrome:</strong> Standard cloud chatbots generate verbose, overly formal paragraphs (<em>"I hope this message finds you well..."</em>) that sound completely unnatural in instant messaging.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Context Switching & Latency Friction:</strong> Copy-pasting conversation threads between messaging apps and ChatGPT browser tabs breaks chat flow and takes more time than typing manually.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Privacy & Corporate Data Leakage:</strong> Sending confidential work chats or personal conversations to third-party cloud servers introduces significant privacy and compliance risks.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Layout Obstruction & Editor Incompatibilities:</strong> Standard browser extensions frequently overlap chat buttons or cause duplicate text insertions inside modern React, Lexical, and Slate messaging editors.
  </li>
</ol>

<h2 className="font-cormorant text-2xl text-zinc-100 font-semibold pt-4 mb-2">2. Supported Messaging Platforms</h2>
