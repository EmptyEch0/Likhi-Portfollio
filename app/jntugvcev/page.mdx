<h2 className="font-cormorant text-2xl text-zinc-100 font-semibold pt-4 mb-2">1. The Problem: The Bottlenecks of Traditional University Websites</h2>

Government and autonomous university portals historically suffer from severe architectural limitations:

<ol className="space-y-3 text-zinc-300 pl-5 list-decimal">
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Centralized Admin Bottlenecks:</strong> Any change — from a departmental seminar circular to updating a professor's email or lab photo — required submitting manual paper requests to a single overburdened webmaster, creating weeks of delay.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Exam-Time Traffic Spikes & Server Crashes:</strong> When semester timetables, hall tickets, or results were posted, thousands of concurrent student requests hit un-cached database queries, leading to server downtime.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Fragmented Department Silos:</strong> Each department (IT, CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Metallurgy, Basic Sciences) lacked the ability to showcase its own vision, research labs, faculty publications, and student societies dynamically.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-100">Poor Mobile Accessibility & Student Discovery:</strong> Cluttered nested menus made finding syllabus regulations, academic calendars, and fee notices nearly impossible for students navigating on mobile devices.
  </li>
</ol>

<h2 className="font-cormorant text-2xl text-zinc-100 font-semibold pt-4 mb-2">3. High-Performance Caching & Media Pipeline</h2>

To deliver instantaneous page loads even during peak traffic periods, the platform implements a layered caching and optimization strategy:

<ul className="space-y-3 text-zinc-300 pl-4 list-disc">
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-200">Stale-While-Revalidate (SWR) Query Caching:</strong> Frequently accessed public endpoints (circulars, departmental faculty rosters, and latest notices) are served instantly from memory cache while revalidating in the background, yielding <strong className="text-zinc-100">&lt;50ms response times</strong>.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-200">Optimized Local Asset Pipeline:</strong> Images and documents uploaded by HODs and staff are routed through an automated compression pipeline (<code className="text-xs bg-zinc-800 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-zinc-300">/local-assets/uploads/</code>), converting bulky uploads into responsive, modern WebP and optimized JPEG formats.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-200">Edge Static Generation for Stable Pages:</strong> Academic regulations, campus history, administrative charts, and facilities pages are pre-rendered at the edge for sub-second global delivery.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-200">Zero-Downtime Database Architecture:</strong> High-efficiency queries constructed via Drizzle ORM over PostgreSQL with connection pooling prevent thread starvation during peak exam notice spikes.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 className="font-cormorant text-2xl text-zinc-100 font-semibold pt-4 mb-2">4. Offline Database-Powered RAG AI Chatbot</h2>

To assist students navigating complex college regulations, we integrated an intelligent conversational assistant:

<ul className="space-y-2 text-zinc-300 pl-4 list-disc">
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-200">Grounded Knowledge Base:</strong> Indexes university regulations, syllabus documents, department faculty directories, lab locations, fee structures, and bus route information.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong className="text-zinc-200">Natural Language Student Inquiries:</strong> Students can query <em>"When are the R20 3-2 mid exams?"</em>, <em>"Who is the HOD of IT department?"</em>, or <em>"How do I apply for a duplicate grade card?"</em> and receive instant, cited answers.
  </li>
  <li>

  </li>
</ul>
